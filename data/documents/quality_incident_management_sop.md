# Quality Incident Management SOP

Critical incidents require immediate containment, notification to Quality and Procurement, segregation of affected stock and a supplier response within 24 hours. Root-cause analysis is due within five business days unless an extension is approved.

High severity incidents require containment within one business day and corrective action tracking. Two High incidents from the same supplier within 90 days trigger Level 2 vendor escalation.

Closure requires documented root cause, corrective action evidence and Quality approval.

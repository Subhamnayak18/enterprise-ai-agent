# Procurement SOP

Procurement must use approved suppliers for standard purchases. Requisitions should include specification, quantity, required date and cost centre. Competitive quotation is required for non-contracted purchases above INR 250,000 unless a documented sole-source exception is approved.

Before award, buyers must check supplier status, current performance issues and unresolved critical quality incidents. Deviations from an approved sourcing decision require manager approval and an audit note.

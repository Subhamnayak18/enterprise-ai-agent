# Inventory Replenishment Policy

Replenishment parameters should consider forecast demand, supplier lead time, service target and demand/lead-time variability. Safety stock changes above 20% require planner review. Emergency replenishment should not be used to hide recurring supplier performance issues; repeated expedite demand must be included in supplier review.

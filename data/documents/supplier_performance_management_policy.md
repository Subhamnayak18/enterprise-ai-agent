# Supplier Performance Management Policy

Supplier scorecards are reviewed monthly using OTD, fill rate, defect rate, quality score and unresolved incidents. A supplier should be placed on Watchlist when two or more major KPIs remain outside threshold for two consecutive review periods.

A supplier rated High or Critical by an internal risk assessment must be reviewed by a procurement manager before new high-value commitments. Risk scores are decision support only and do not automatically suspend a supplier.

Removal from Watchlist requires two consecutive compliant monthly reviews and closure of overdue corrective actions.

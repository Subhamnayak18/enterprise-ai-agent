# Vendor Escalation Policy

Level 1 is a buyer-led review for a first material breach. The supplier must acknowledge the issue within two business days and provide recovery timing.

Level 2 is led by the category manager when a material KPI breach repeats, a corrective action is overdue, or two High severity incidents occur within 90 days. A documented corrective action plan and weekly status review are required.

Level 3 requires procurement head and quality head review when there is a Critical quality incident, repeated Level 2 failure, or an immediate continuity/compliance threat. Possible actions include controlled order allocation, enhanced incoming inspection, temporary sourcing hold, or suspension recommendation. Suspension requires human approval; the AI assistant must never execute it automatically.

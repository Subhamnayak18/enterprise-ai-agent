# Supplier Delivery SLA

## Service levels
Strategic suppliers are expected to maintain monthly on-time delivery (OTD) of at least 95%. An OTD result from 90% to below 95% triggers a performance review. OTD below 90% in any month requires a corrective action plan within five business days.

## Repeated breach
Two consecutive months below 90% requires Level 2 escalation to the category manager. Three months below 90% within a rolling six-month period requires a formal supplier recovery plan and weekly tracking. Procurement may recommend temporary allocation reduction if service risk threatens production continuity.

## Measurement
A delivery is on time when the accepted receipt date is no later than the confirmed expected delivery date. Customer-caused schedule changes are excluded when documented.

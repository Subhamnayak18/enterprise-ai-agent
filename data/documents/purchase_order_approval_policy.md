# Purchase Order Approval Policy

Purchase orders up to INR 250,000 require buyer approval. Orders above INR 250,000 and up to INR 1,000,000 require category manager approval. Orders above INR 1,000,000 require procurement head approval.

A new purchase order for a Watchlist supplier above INR 500,000 requires category manager acknowledgement of the active supplier risk before approval. Critical risk or an unresolved Critical quality incident requires procurement head and Quality review before commitment.
